# v0.4 examples: results worked out by hand

These are the expected results for the v0.4 examples, computed by hand before the implementation existed. The `.cases.json` files must match them. Calendar facts used: 2026-01-01 is a Thursday, so 2026-11-02 is a Monday (305 days later, 305 mod 7 = 4); 2026 is not a leap year; 2024 is.

## `learn/types_and_enums.grph`

| priority | team | minutes_open | sla_minutes | breached | escalate | team out | label |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Tech | 20 | 15 | true (20 > 15) | true (Urgent and breached) | Tech | `Urgent for Tech` |
| Low | Sales | 100 | 2880 | false | false | Billing (Sales goes to Billing) | `Low for Sales` |
| Normal | Billing | 240 | 240 | false (240 > 240 is false) | false | Billing | `Normal for Billing` |

A `priority` of `"Critical"` is not a variant: the input is rejected with R005 before the program runs.

## `learn/dates.grph`

Issued 2026-10-01, due 2026-10-31, so `term_days` = 30 in every row.

| today | days_between(due, today) | branch | days_late | remind_on | remind_weekday |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-12 | 12 | overdue (12 > 5) | 12 | 2026-11-19 | 4 (Nov 12 is a Thursday, +7 days) |
| 2026-11-03 | 3 | on time (3 ≤ 5) | 3 | 2026-10-31 (the due date) | 6 (Oct 31 is a Saturday: Nov 2 Monday − 2 days) |
| 2026-10-20 | −11 | on time | 0 (`max(−11, 0)`) | 2026-10-31 | 6 |

Leap year: issued 2024-02-28, due 2024-03-01 gives `term_days` = 2 (Feb 29 exists in 2024). With today = 2024-03-01 the invoice is on time, `remind_on` is the due date, and `remind_weekday` is 5 (a Friday).

## `apps/payroll.grph` (with `lib/payroll.grph`)

The arithmetic is the same as `apps/payroll_run.grph`, with `es`, `remote-eu` and `remote-us` renamed to the variants `Spain`, `RemoteEu` and `RemoteUs`:

| Employee | Hours × rate | Gross | Tax | Net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ana, Spain (20%, credit 50.00) | 172 × 25.00 | 160 × 25 + 12 × 25 × 1.5 = 4450.00 | 890.00 | 4450 − 890 + 50 = 3610.00 |
| bo, RemoteEu (15%, credit 0.00) | 160 × 30.00 | 4800.00 | 720.00 | 4080.00 |
| cy, RemoteUs (10%, credit 25.00) | 40 × 20.00 | 800.00 | 80.00 | 745.00 |

- **Funded run** (period 2026-10, balance 1,000,000 cents = 10000.00, approver Cfo): total 8435.00, three transfers, `approved_by` = `"Cfo"`, largest 4080.00, nothing skipped.
- **Short run** (period 2026-11, balance 500.00): fails with InsufficientFunds and alerts "payroll 2026-11 is short by 7935.00" (500.00 − 8435.00 = −7935.00).
- **Intern approver**: fails with NotApproved.
- **Tax service down** (a mock whose `TaxTable` always answers `TableDown`): fails with TaxServiceDown.
- **Frozen period** (2026-12): fails with PeriodFrozen before any effect.
- **Empty staff list**: fails with NothingToPay.
- **A region that is not a variant** (`"Mars"`): rejected at the input with R005, so the program no longer needs an `UnknownRegion` error.
